We collect and process the following categories of information when you interact with our website, the Kore App or our services:

2.1 Contact Data

  • Information provided through the contact form: name, email address, subject and message
  • Correspondence information when we contact you by email

2.2 Email Subscription Data

  • Email address to receive our newsletter and welcome sequences
  • Subscription preferences and mailing list status
  • Email interactions (opens, clicks, replies)

2.3 Usage and Activity Data

  • Information about how you browse and use the website

2.4 Advertising Data

  • Information about cookies and advertising pixels used by Google AdSense and third-party ad networks
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • Advertising ID and interest data for personalized ads

2.5 Automatically Collected Data

  • Cookies and similar technologies (see section 7)
  • Device and connection usage data
  • Data collected by third-party services (Firebase Analytics, etc.)

3. Legal Basis for Data Processing

The processing of your personal information is based on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent: When you explicitly give us permission to collect or use your data, such as when you subscribe to our newsletter or accept cookies.
  • Legitimate interest: When we use your data to improve our services, analyze site performance, personalize your experience, show relevant ads, or for security purposes.
  • Performance of a contract: When it is necessary to provide you a requested service, such as responding to an inquiry through the contact form.
  • Legal obligation: When we are required by law to process your data, such as in compliance with tax regulations.

4. What Do We Use Your Data For?

We use the information collected through the website, the Kore App and our services to:

  • Show personalized ads: Use Google AdSense and third-party ad networks to show relevant ads based on your browsing activity.
  • Measure advertising performance: Track ad impressions, clicks and conversions to optimize our campaigns.
  • Improve our services: Analyze usage patterns, develop new features and personalize the user experience.
  • Security and fraud prevention: Detect and prevent unauthorized access, protect the integrity of our services.
  • Comply with legal obligations: Respond to legal requirements and comply with tax and regulatory regulations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share data with third parties that help us operate our website, the Kore App and our services, and that comply with strict data protection measures:

Google may also collect information about your visits to this site and other sites in order to show ads about products and services that may be of interest to you.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to show ads on our site. 5.3 Technical Providers

  • Web hosting servers.
  • Analytics and usage metrics providers.

5.4 Legal Disclosure

We may disclose your data if we are required by law, a court order, a government request, or to protect our rights, property or safety, or those of our users and the public.

Brevo operates under the EU-US Privacy Shield framework for personal data transfers from the EU. Google AdSense processes user data on servers located in the United States in accordance with Google’s policies.

We keep your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

We use cookies and similar technologies on the website and the Kore App to:

  • Essential cookies: For the operation of the website (session, authentication, language preferences).
  • Analytics cookies: Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with our website and the Kore App.
  • Advertising cookies: Google AdSense and third-party ad networks use cookies to show personalized ads, measure campaign effectiveness and avoid showing the same ads repeatedly.
  • Preference cookies: To remember your preferred language and settings.
  • Marketing cookies: To show relevant content and measure email campaigns (only with consent).

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings. Keep in mind that by disabling essential cookies, parts of our website may not work correctly. You can also manage your cookie preferences with Brevo through their privacy policy.

Opt-out option: You can opt out of personalized Google AdSense ads by visiting Google Ads Settings or downloading the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. You can also opt out of personalized ads from other ad networks through the OptOut.org or the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out tool.

10. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / LOPD)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Request and receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to erasure: Request the deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a structured format and request its transfer to another controller.
  • Right to object: Object to the processing of your data, especially for direct marketing and personalized advertising.
  • Right to restrict processing: Restrict the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right not to be subject to automated decisions: Not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of previous processing.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: Contact your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, send a request to alquimialab88@gmail.com. We will respond within the next 30 business days. We will verify your identity before processing any request.

  • Right to opt out of the sale: Object to the sale of your personal data to third parties. You can exercise this right by clicking "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" or contacting us at alquimialab88@gmail.com.
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of personal data we have collected from you.
  • Right not to be discriminated against: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights under the CCPA.

This website does not sell personal data to third parties. Data collected through Firebase Analytics and Google AdSense is used solely for analytical and advertising purposes, in compliance with Google’s policies.

When using the Kore App, we collect the following specific data:
