The story

Why Kore?

Kore was born from a personal need: finding calm in the middle of chaos. Its creator wanted one app to order her day and care for her mind — since it didn't exist, she built it.

In Ancient Greek, Kore (κόρη) means “maiden”: the name of Persephone as goddess of spring — a symbol of rebirth and starting anew.

Kore is that: your daily rebirth, your space to come back to yourself.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Kore is free to start your daily system. Optional premium features come later — the base will always be free.

Do I need to use it every day?

No guilt here. Miss a day? Come back the next. The tracker shows real progress — not a streak that stresses you out.

Does it work if I have ADHD or anxiety?

It was designed with that brain in mind: few decisions, clear rituals, zero scolding. It doesn't replace therapy — it supports your system.
