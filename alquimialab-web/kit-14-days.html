Before you start

How to use this kit

Not a perfect week — 14 days with anchors. Print everything, pick real hours, honor the 5 minutes. Chaos leaves with system, not willpower.

  1. Print the pages and keep them in a folder or binder. Ideally the Sunday before.
  2. Pick your anchor: one fixed hour per ritual. No fantasy schedules.
  3. Every night mark the tracker AND fill your reflection: what did I learn? how did I feel?
  4. If heavy chaos hits → go to Panic protocol (p. 11). Don't improvise.
  5. Week 2 is maintenance: same anchors, lighter load. That's where the real habit installs.
  6. Day 14: design your forever weekly template. What worked stays.
You don't need to master your whole life. Just these 5 minutes, 14 days in a row.

Panic protocol

When anxiety shows up unannounced at 2am or mid-meeting — DON'T think, just follow these steps in order.

Grounding 5 · 4 · 3 · 2 · 1

Anchor to what's here, now. Your mind can't do both at once (panic AND count).

  1. 5 things you can SEE — name them out loud or in your head
  2. 4 things you can TOUCH — table, clothes, floor
  3. 3 things you can HEAR — traffic, fan, your breath
  4. 2 things you can SMELL — your clothes, the air
  5. 1 thing you can TASTE — the taste in your mouth, a sip of water
This is not weakness — it's your emergency system. Use it guilt-free. Then come back to the kit when you're ready.
If it doesn't ease in 10 min

Cold water on your face or wrists · change physical space · talk to someone you trust · if it's recurrent, getting professional help isn't failing — it's part of the system. 💜
