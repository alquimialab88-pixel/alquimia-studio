The book · Synopsis

I quit, I trembled… and Alquimia was born.

The story of El diario imperfecto de una creadora — what happens inside its pages, and why you'll want to read it to the last exercise.

This is not an expert's book. It's my imperfect diary.

One night I had had enough: $8 posts, gifted nights, and a heart that was done. So I quit — and then I trembled in front of the empty calendar.

From that rubble Alquimia was born: a one-woman studio built from an anime, a feeling and a lot of guerrilla resourcefulness.

And then the real battle began: learning to work with AI instead of fighting it — from sworn enemy to trusted assistant, and why a pretty logo made by AI doesn't make a brand.

Inside you'll find real chapters, unfinished endings, 15 prompts to copy and paste, exercises with space to write, and a launch checklist — the map I wish I'd had.

No smoke here. Just the real thing.
